## 🧑‍💻 A Bit About Me

I'm a Sri Lanka-based Software Engineer who likes taking messy requirements and turning them into systems that hold up under real load — banking features, booking platforms, document pipelines, the kind of software where "it mostly works" isn't good enough.

Day to day, that means **C#, ASP.NET Core, and SQL Server**. Outside of that, I'm pulling apart **AI agents and generative AI** to see what actually belongs in production software versus what's just a good demo.

> "Build software that's simple enough to understand, strong enough to scale, and flexible enough to evolve."
